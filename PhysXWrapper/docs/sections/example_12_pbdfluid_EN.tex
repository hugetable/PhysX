\subsection{Description}
\textbf{PBD Fluids} simulate particle-based fluids using Position Based Dynamics on GPU. Applications: water simulation, liquid effects, SPH fluids.

\subsubsection{Physics: PBD Solver}
Iterative constraint projection:
\begin{equation}
\mathbf{x}_{i+1} = \mathbf{x}_i + \Delta\mathbf{x}_i, \quad \Delta\mathbf{x}_i = \frac{\lambda_i}{\sum m_j} \nabla C_i
\end{equation}
